WHAT WE DO

Practical financial support from records to strategy.

Strong financial management starts with accurate records, effective processes and a clear understanding of your business position.

GLB Consults provides a range of accounting, tax, payroll, inventory and financial advisory services designed to help businesses operate more efficiently.

OUR CORE SERVICES

Everything you need to strengthen your finances.

01

Accounting & Bookkeeping

We help businesses maintain accurate and well-organised financial records through transaction recording, financial reporting, bank reconciliations and accounts payable and receivable management.

02

Tax Management

We provide support with tax registration, advisory and statutory obligations including VAT, CIT, PAYE and WHT, helping businesses stay organised and compliant.

03

Inventory Count

We support periodic physical stock counts, inventory valuation and reconciliation while identifying opportunities to improve stock control and management.

04

Internal Controls

We review existing controls, identify gaps and risks, and recommend practical improvements that can strengthen financial and operational processes.

05

Financial Systems Setup

We help businesses select and set up suitable accounting systems such as QuickBooks, Sage and Zoho Books, including configuration, migration and user training.

06

Payroll Management

We support monthly payroll preparation, reconciliation and payslip processing, alongside relevant PAYE, pension, NHF and other statutory obligations.

ADDITIONAL ADVISORY

Professional guidance beyond the numbers.

Businesses sometimes need more than routine accounting. GLB Consults also provides support with management reporting, audit preparation, IFRS-related advisory and financial process documentation.

We can also assist with process improvements and standard operating procedures that help businesses create more organised and reliable financial operations.
